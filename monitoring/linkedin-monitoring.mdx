LinkedIn Monitoring gives you an inbox for LinkedIn engagement on your company pages. It has two tabs:

- **Comments**: inbound comments on your company page posts
- **Mentions**: posts by others that mention your company page

From either tab you can reply from a connected profile, react, and mark items as resolved without opening LinkedIn.

<Note>
  LinkedIn Monitoring is rolling out gradually. If you don't see **Monitoring** in your sidebar, it isn't enabled for your workspace yet.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- A connected LinkedIn **company page**. Ordinal monitors comments on your company page posts and mentions of your company page.
- To reply or react, at least one connected LinkedIn profile with **like** or **comment** engagement permissions. See [Connecting LinkedIn](/social-profiles/linkedin#connecting-linkedin) for how to grant engagement permissions.

## The comments inbox

Open **LinkedIn Monitoring** from the sidebar and select the **Comments** tab. The left panel lists your company page posts that have comments, sorted by most recent activity. Each post shows:

- The company page and a preview of the post text
- A progress bar with a resolved count (for example, `2/5`)
- A checkmark once every comment on the post is resolved

Select a post to open its comment threads. Each thread shows the root comment and its replies. Use **View on LinkedIn** to open the original post on LinkedIn.

Replies from your own company page don't count toward a post's comment totals, so the resolved count reflects only inbound comments.

## Resolving comments

Resolving is how you track which comments still need attention. Resolved comments stay in the thread but appear muted.

- Select **Resolve** on a comment to mark it handled.
- Open the comment actions menu on a resolved comment and select **Unresolve Comment** to reopen it.
- Replying to a comment resolves it automatically.

Resolved state is tracked per workspace and doesn't change anything on LinkedIn.

## Replying to comments

1. Open a thread and select the reply action on the comment you want to answer.
2. Pick which connected profile to reply as using the actor selector. Any connected LinkedIn profile with comment engagement permissions can reply, including the company page itself.
3. Write your reply and send it.

Ordinal publishes the reply to LinkedIn in-thread and marks the comment you replied to as resolved.

## Reacting to comments

Use the reaction picker on any comment to react as a connected profile. LinkedIn Monitoring supports all LinkedIn reaction types:

- Like
- Celebrate
- Love
- Insightful
- Support
- Funny

Selecting a different reaction replaces your existing one. Selecting the same reaction again removes it.

## The mentions inbox

Switch to the **Mentions** tab to see posts by others that mention your company page. The left panel lists company mentions, sorted by most recent activity. Resolved mentions appear muted.

Select a mention to see the source post, who published it, and when. Use **View on LinkedIn** to open the post on LinkedIn.

From the mention detail you can:

- **Resolve or unresolve** the mention to track what still needs attention. Resolved state is tracked per workspace and doesn't change anything on LinkedIn.
- **Comment on the post** as a connected profile using the actor selector. Commenting on a mention resolves it automatically.
- **React to the post** using the same reaction picker as the comments inbox.
