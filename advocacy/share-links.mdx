Advocacy share links let you invite a teammate to post a story to their personal LinkedIn from a page they open outside Ordinal. Recipients don't need an Ordinal account — they sign in with LinkedIn on the share page and either post right away or pick a time to publish.

## How share links work

1. An admin creates a shareable link for a story and sends it to a recipient.
2. The recipient opens the link, signs in with LinkedIn, and reviews the story.
3. They select **Share to LinkedIn**, then either post immediately or schedule a future time.
4. When the scheduled time arrives, Ordinal publishes the post to the recipient's LinkedIn profile.

## Scheduling a share

On the share page, select **Share to LinkedIn** to open the schedule modal.

- **Post now** — leave the time set to the current time (or within about 5 seconds of it) and select **Share**. The post publishes immediately.
- **Schedule for later** — pick any future date and time. The share page shows a scheduled banner with the publish time in your timezone.

After scheduling, use the overflow menu on the scheduled banner to:

- **Edit scheduled time** — reopen the modal and pick a new date and time.
- **Unschedule** — cancel the scheduled post. The publish job is cancelled and nothing goes out.

Once the post publishes, the share page shows a **Posted** state with a link to the live LinkedIn post.

## Reposts of a company LinkedIn post

If the story is a repost of a company LinkedIn post that hasn't gone live yet, scheduling has two guardrails:

- You can't pick a time before the company post is scheduled to publish. The date picker's minimum is set to the company post's scheduled time.
- The schedule modal suggests a random time 1–20 minutes after the company post so the reshare doesn't land before the source is live.

If a recipient schedules their share for a valid future time, Ordinal waits until the source company post is live before publishing the reshare. No action is needed from the recipient — the job retries automatically until the source URN is available.

<Note>
  When you create a share link for a story whose company repost is still scheduled, the create modal warns you that recipients will only be able to schedule after the company post goes live.
</Note>
