## Overview

Once connected, your assistant has access to the tools below. You don't need to call them by hand — just ask in plain language ("draft a LinkedIn post for Friday," "show me last month's engagement") and the assistant picks the right tool.

:::info
**Always start with workspaces.** If this is a new session, ask the assistant to "list my Ordinal workspaces" first. It needs to know which workspace you want before it can do anything else.
:::

## Discovery

| Tool | What it does |
|------|---------|
| `ordinal_get_workspace_context` | Lists your workspaces, then (once you pick one) returns the profiles, labels, and users the assistant needs to do everything else. The assistant requests only the sections it needs and can filter profiles by channel or name, so large workspaces stay within response limits. |

## Posts and ideas

| Tool | What it does |
|------|---------|
| `ordinal_search_content` | Searches posts or ideas — by status, labels, campaigns, or date — and can fetch a single post with its comments. Every post and idea it returns includes its [notes](/posts/overview#notes). |
| `ordinal_create_post` | Creates a post with LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, and/or YouTube Shorts content, optionally with a workflow status. |
| `ordinal_create_idea` | Creates an idea (a draft without a scheduled publish time). |
| `ordinal_manage_post` | Updates, archives, or unarchives a post, including changing its workflow status. |
| `ordinal_manage_idea` | Updates, archives, unarchives, or adds an idea to the calendar. |
| `ordinal_list_post_versions` | Lists saved [versions](/posts/version-history) of a post's draft per channel, with the author and timestamp of each. |
| `ordinal_get_post_version_content` | Reads the copy of a single saved version, so the assistant can diff two revisions or study how a draft evolved. |

### Notes

`ordinal_create_post`, `ordinal_create_idea`, `ordinal_manage_post`, and `ordinal_manage_idea` can write a post's or idea's [notes](/posts/overview#notes), and `ordinal_search_content` returns them, so the assistant can read the internal context your team left on a draft. Posts and ideas without notes return `null`, and setting `notes` to `null` with a manage tool clears them.

Notes don't support mentions. If the assistant writes a mention into a note, Ordinal keeps the person's name as plain text.

For example, ask "what notes did we leave on the pricing launch post?" and the assistant calls `ordinal_search_content` to fetch the post and read its notes.

### Post statuses

`ordinal_create_post` and `ordinal_manage_post` accept a `status` of `Tentative`, `ToDo`, `InProgress`, `ForReview`, or `Scheduled`, so the assistant can move posts through the same [workflow statuses](/posts/statuses) you use in the app. New posts default to `ToDo`. `Posted` can't be set through MCP because Ordinal applies it only after a post publishes.

For example, ask "move my draft about the pricing launch to For Review" and the assistant calls `ordinal_manage_post` with `status: "ForReview"`.

## Media

| Tool | What it does |
|------|---------|
| `ordinal_create_upload` | Uploads an image, video, or PDF from a URL, or prepares signed credentials so a local file can be uploaded. PDFs are LinkedIn-only and publish as document (carousel) posts. |
| `ordinal_get_upload` | Checks whether an upload is ready. |

For a local file, the assistant calls `ordinal_create_upload` with `filename`, `mimetype`, and `size`, POSTs the file to the returned `uploadUrl` with `params` and `signature`, then polls `ordinal_get_upload` until status is `ready`.

## Amplify

| Tool | What it does |
|------|---------|
| `ordinal_search_amplify_links` | Searches Amplify links (draft, live, or completed) by state, label, or creator, or fetches one by ID. With `includeContent`, it also returns each action's target post and copy variants with their media. |
| `ordinal_list_amplify_engagers` | Lists the people who completed actions on a published Amplify link — one entry per person with their name, headline, LinkedIn profile URL, and every like, comment, repost, or post they completed, each with a permalink. |
| `ordinal_create_amplify_link` | Creates a draft Amplify link in one call: title, notes, labels, and actions. |
| `ordinal_manage_amplify_link` | Updates, publishes, or deletes an existing Amplify link, and adds or removes its actions and copy variants. |

All Amplify tools require Amplify to be enabled on your workspace. Like permalinks point at the campaign's target post; comment, repost, and post permalinks point at the engager's own content.

`ordinal_list_amplify_engagers` is paginated (default 50 per page, max 100) and accepts an optional `action` filter. The filter only narrows which people are returned — each person's list of actions still includes everything they completed on the link.

For example, ask "who engaged with our launch Amplify link?" and the assistant calls `ordinal_search_amplify_links` to find the link, then `ordinal_list_amplify_engagers` to return names and permalinks your team can follow up on.

### Creating and editing links

`ordinal_create_amplify_link` always creates a **draft**. Drafts aren't sent to anyone until they're published. Each action is one of:

- **Engage** — recipients like, comment on, or repost a target post. Point it at an Ordinal post ID (scheduled or already published) or a LinkedIn post URL from one of your workspace's profiles, and choose which of like, comment, and repost to allow. Reposts need at least one copy variant.
- **Create post** — recipients publish their own post, starting from one of your copy variants. Variants can include media uploaded with `ordinal_create_upload` (see [Media](#media)).

The assistant checks every input before writing anything, so an invalid action never leaves a half-built link behind.

`ordinal_manage_amplify_link` takes one of these actions:

| Action | What it does |
|------|---------|
| `update` | Changes the title, notes, labels, engagement settings, target post, or variant copy and media. Only the fields you mention change; nothing left out is deleted. |
| `add_variant` / `remove_variant` | Adds or removes a copy variant on an action. |
| `add_action` / `remove_action` | Adds or removes an action. Drafts only. |
| `publish` | Creates the short link and makes the link live. Requires an end date; the share title and short link slug default to ones built from the link title. |
| `delete` | Deletes a draft. Published links can't be deleted this way. |

Because this tool can change or delete links, most assistants ask you to approve each call. Once a link is published, you can still change its engagement settings, copy, and variants, but not its target posts or actions. Changing a variant's copy on a live link also updates it in recipients' drafts that haven't been scheduled yet.

Both tools return the link with an `editUrl` that opens it in Ordinal; `ordinal_manage_amplify_link` also returns the `shareUrl` to send to your team once the link is published.

For example, ask "create an Amplify link asking the sales team to like and repost our launch post, with two repost variants" and the assistant creates the draft and shares the edit link. Then say "publish it, ending Friday at 5pm" to make it live.

## LinkedIn Monitoring

| Tool | What it does |
|------|---------|
| `ordinal_list_linkedin_monitoring_posts` | Lists inbox posts with unresolved comment counts, or fetches a single post's metadata by ID. |
| `ordinal_list_linkedin_monitoring_comments` | Returns paginated root comments (with their replies) on an inbox post. |
| `ordinal_manage_linkedin_monitoring_comments` | Replies to a comment, reacts to it on LinkedIn, or resolves and unresolves it in the inbox. |
| `ordinal_list_linkedin_monitoring_mentions` | Lists inbound @mentions of your connected pages, or fetches a single mention by ID. |
| `ordinal_manage_linkedin_monitoring_mentions` | Comments on a mentioning post, reacts to it, or resolves and unresolves the mention. |

These tools appear only for workspaces where LinkedIn Monitoring is enabled, and they act on the same inbox you see in the app. Comments are paginated 10 roots per page by default (max 50); posts and mentions are 20 per page (max 50). Replies and reactions are posted from a scheduling profile — get the `profileId` from `ordinal_get_workspace_context`.

Replying to a comment automatically resolves that comment. Replying to a reply still posts on the thread root. Commenting on a mention does *not* auto-resolve it — resolve separately when you're done. LinkedIn reactions accept `Like`, `Celebrate`, `Love`, `Insightful`, `Support`, or `Funny`; sending the same reaction again removes it.

For example, ask "triage today's LinkedIn comments — reply to anything from a customer and resolve the rest" and the assistant lists unresolved posts, pages through comments, replies where needed, and marks the others resolved.

`ordinal_get_linkedin_mention` under [Platform utilities](#platform-utilities) is different: it looks up a LinkedIn user or company URN to mention in post copy, not an inbox notification.

## Asking the assistant things

You don't call these tools by name — you describe what you want. Examples:

- "Create a LinkedIn post for next Tuesday about our new pricing, in the Launch campaign."
- "Archive the drafts I wrote last month that don't have a label."
- "Show me which posts are waiting on approval from Sarah."
- "Give me the last 30 days of follower growth on LinkedIn."
- "Show me how the LinkedIn copy on this post changed between the last two saves."
- "List everyone who commented through our launch Amplify link, with links to their comments."
- "Create a draft Amplify link asking the team to repost our product launch post."
- "Reply to any unresolved LinkedIn comments on our launch post, then resolve the rest."

The assistant picks the tools, fills in the arguments, and shows you the result.
