Monitoring is an inbox for every comment and @mention on your LinkedIn company pages. Open **Monitoring** in the sidebar to read each thread, reply or react as any connected profile, and mark items resolved once they're handled, without leaving Ordinal.

<Frame>
  ![LinkedIn Monitoring inbox with a comment thread open](/images/monitoring/linkedin-inbox.png)
</Frame>

:::note
**Plan availability:** Monitoring is included on the Growth plan and turned on by default. On other plans, Ordinal can enable it for your workspace. Email [support@tryordinal.com](mailto:support@tryordinal.com) to request it.
:::

## Requirements

- At least one LinkedIn **company page** connected as a scheduling profile. See [Connecting LinkedIn](/social-profiles/linkedin#connecting-linkedin).
- Personal profiles are not monitored. Only company pages appear in the inbox.

## Comments

The **Comments** tab shows every post on your company pages that has comments, with the newest activity first. Each row is a post. Open it to see the full thread.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a post">
    Select a post in the **Comments** tab to read its thread. Comments that include an image show the image, and links in comments are clickable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a profile to respond as">
    Pick which connected profile to respond as. You can reply as the company page or as a teammate's connected personal profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reply or react">
    Write a reply in the thread or add a reaction. Replying to a comment marks that comment resolved.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve what's done">
    Mark a comment resolved when it no longer needs a response. Resolved comments leave the default view.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mentions

The **Mentions** tab lists every LinkedIn post that @mentions one of your company pages.

<Frame>
  ![Mentions tab listing posts that mention a company page](/images/monitoring/linkedin-mentions.png)
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a mention">
    Select a mention to open the source post.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Comment or react">
    Comment on the post or add a reaction as any connected profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve it">
    Commenting or reacting on a mention does not resolve it. Mark the mention resolved when you're done with it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resolved items

Resolved comments and mentions are hidden by default, so the inbox only shows what still needs a response. The counts on the **Comments** and **Mentions** tabs show how many items are open.

Turn on **Show resolved** to bring resolved items back, for example to review how a thread was handled.

## Filter by company page

If you manage more than one company page, use the profile filter at the top of the inbox to narrow it to a single page.

## How the inbox stays current

Ordinal keeps the inbox up to date through LinkedIn's notifications, so new comments and mentions arrive on their own. When you connect or reconnect a company page, Ordinal backfills the last 60 days of comments and mentions.

## Monitoring with MCP

The same inbox is available to AI assistants through the [Ordinal MCP server](/mcp/introduction). From Claude or any other MCP client, you can list posts with open comments, read threads, reply, react, resolve and unresolve, and do the same for mentions. The Monitoring tools appear only when Monitoring is enabled for your workspace.

See the [LinkedIn Monitoring tools](/mcp/tools#linkedin-monitoring) for the full list.
