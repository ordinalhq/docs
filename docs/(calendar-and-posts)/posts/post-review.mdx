Post Review is an AI proofreader that reads your draft and flags objective mistakes you wouldn't want to publish - typos, grammar slips, and leftover placeholder text. It runs automatically when you open it, so you can catch issues without breaking flow.

## When to use it

Use Post Review when you want a quick second pass before scheduling or publishing:

- Right before you set a publish time, from the schedule modal
- Anytime while editing, from the post header
- On longer posts where small fixes have the biggest impact

Post Review never edits your post. It only surfaces suggestions - you decide what to apply.

## Running a review

You can run a review from two places:

| Location           | How to open                                                       |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Schedule modal     | Click **Schedule Post**. The review section appears in the modal. |
| Post header        | Click **Post Review** in the post header.                         |

In both places, the review runs automatically the first time you open it. When it finishes, you'll see either **No suggestions** or a count like **3 Suggestions Found**.

Expand the section to view each suggestion. Click **Re-run** to generate a fresh review after you make edits.

## What Post Review checks

Post Review reads the copy for every channel on the post, plus the copy of any Comment or Repost engagements. It flags:

- **Spelling mistakes**: misspelled words, missing apostrophes (`dont`, `the companys plan`), doubled words (`to to`), words run together, and sound-alike mix-ups (`loose` for `lose`, `then` for `than`)
- **Grammar mistakes**: a missing or extra small word, the wrong verb form, subject-verb disagreement, the wrong article (`a hour`), or the wrong singular or plural
- **Leftover placeholders**: template text like `[INSERT LINK]` or `[Company name]`, `XX` or `TK` standing in for a number or name, and filler like Lorem ipsum
- **Stray sentences**: a line that's clearly left over from a different draft or topic
- **LinkedIn link previews**: a preview that's about a different article than the post, or a malformed preview URL

Social copy is meant to be informal, so Post Review leaves your voice alone. It doesn't flag:

- Style, tone, or wording choices, including fragments, lowercase starts, slang, and emoji
- British vs. American spelling (`behaviour` and `behavior` are both fine)
- How you capitalize or spell brands, products, people, and handles, or industry jargon
- Punctuation and formatting, such as commas, dashes, quote styles, spacing, and line breaks
- Stats, claims, and dates

## Suggestions

Each suggestion includes:

- A short title and an icon showing whether it's an error or a warning
- A one-line description that quotes the mistake and the fix, for example: "dont" should be "don't"

Each distinct mistake gets its own suggestion, so a post with three typos shows three suggestions. Suggestions are advisory. Fix the ones that apply and skip the rest.
