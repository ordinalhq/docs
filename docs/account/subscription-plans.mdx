Ordinal offers flexible plans to fit teams of all sizes, from individual creators to large agencies. This guide covers how billing works and how to manage your subscription.

![Billing](/images/billing.jpg)

## Plans

We offer three tiers designed to scale with your needs:

| Plan           | Best for                   | Highlights                                                                       |
| -------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Starter**    | Individuals                | Up to 3 seats, 4 social profiles, 50 posts/month                                 |
| **Pro**        | Teams                      | Unlimited seats, unlimited posts, team engagements, all-time analytics, API, MCP & webhooks |
| **Enterprise** | Growing teams & large orgs | Custom pricing, security & account rules, CSA/MSA, prioritized support, and more |

For full pricing details, feature comparisons, and current rates, visit our [pricing page](https://www.tryordinal.com/pricing).

:::note
**Agencies and freelancers:** Managing content for multiple clients? We offer bulk discounts and agency-specific features. Chat with us or email [**support@tryordinal.com**](mailto:support@tryordinal.com) to learn more.
:::

## How billing works

- **Billing frequency**: Choose monthly or yearly billing. Annual plans save approximately 10%.
- **Enterprise billing**: Enterprise plans are billed yearly only.

### Adding or removing profiles mid-cycle

- **Adding profiles:** You'll be charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in the current cycle.
- **Removing profiles:** You'll be refunded a prorated amount for the remaining days in the current cycle as credit.

## Managing your billing

### View or change your plan

1. Go to [Settings → Plans](https://app.tryordinal.com/settings/pricing)
2. Compare available plans and select the one you want
3. Confirm the change

Changes to your plan take effect immediately and you'll be charged or refunded the prorated amount for the remaining days in the current cycle.

### Update payment method

1. Go to [**Settings → Billing**](https://app.tryordinal.com/settings/billing)
2. In the **Payment Method** section, select **Update** to add or replace your card or bank account

### View invoices

1. Go to [**Settings → Billing**](https://app.tryordinal.com/settings/billing)
2. Scroll to **Recent Invoices** to view your latest charges, or select **View all invoices** to see the full history
3. Select any invoice to download a PDF copy

### Set a billing email

Payment receipts are sent to the billing email on file. Update it whenever you want receipts to go to a different inbox, such as a shared finance alias.

1. Go to [**Settings → Billing**](https://app.tryordinal.com/settings/billing)
2. In the **Billing Email** section, enter the address you want receipts sent to
3. Select **Save**

To stop sending receipts to a dedicated address, clear the field and select **Save**. Future receipts fall back to the default email on your Stripe customer record.

## Changing or canceling your subscription

### Upgrading

When you upgrade:

- New features are available immediately
- You're charged the prorated difference for the current cycle

### Downgrading

When you downgrade:

- The change takes effect at the end of your billing period
- You keep full access until then
- If you exceed the new plan's limits (e.g., too many social profiles), you'll need to disconnect some before the downgrade takes effect

### Canceling

To cancel your subscription:

1. Go to [**Settings → Billing**](https://app.tryordinal.com/settings/billing)
2. In the **Current Plan** section, select **Cancel Plan**
3. Confirm the cancellation

You can resume a canceled subscription anytime before the billing period ends by selecting **Resume Plan** from the same page.

:::warning
Cancellations take effect at the end of your current billing period. You can reactivate your subscription at any time without losing your data.
:::

After cancellation:

- You retain access until the end of your billing period
- Your data is preserved (nothing is deleted)
- You can reactivate your subscription at any time

## Free trial

New workspaces start with a **14-day free trial** - no credit card required. During the trial, you have full access to Pro features.

To see your trial status, go to [**Settings → Billing**](https://app.tryordinal.com/settings/billing). The trial plan card shows your current tier and expiry date, along with a **Choose a Plan** link. When the trial ends, you can choose a paid plan or continue with limited functionality.

### Choosing a plan when your trial ends

When your trial ends, Ordinal shows **Your free trial has ended**. Select **Choose a plan** to compare plans, then **Select Plan** on the plan you want and review the summary before selecting **Proceed to Payment**. The same flow opens from **Choose a plan** if your subscription becomes inactive.

#### Downgrading to Starter

Starter doesn't include some Pro features. If your workspace is on Pro (or a plan above it) and you choose Starter, selecting **Proceed to Payment** opens a **Downgrading from Pro to Starter?** window before checkout (the title names your current plan). It lists the Pro features your team currently uses and what changes for each:

| Feature | What changes on Starter |
| --- | --- |
| **Team engagements** | Shows how many posts used team engagements in the last 30 days. Teammates can no longer like, comment, or repost from their profiles, and any engagement-only profiles are disconnected. |
| **MCP** | Shows how many team members use MCP. Claude, Codex, and other AI tools lose access to the workspace. |
| **API and webhooks** | Shows how many API keys were used in the last 30 days. Those keys stop working. |
| **All-time analytics** | Shows how many LinkedIn posts your analytics include and how far back they go. Starter only shows the last 14 days. |

Features your team doesn't use are listed under **Also not included in Starter**. If your team uses none of them, the window lists them all under **Features not included in Starter**. Select **View all plan differences** to go back to the plan comparison.

- Select **Downgrade to Starter** to continue to checkout.
- Select **Stay on Pro** to switch the summary to the Pro plan instead.

:::note
Engagement-only profiles aren't billed on Starter. Ordinal disconnects them automatically once your Starter subscription starts. See [Team Engagements](/posts/team-engagements) for what Starter supports.
:::
