Send the short link anywhere: Slack, email, a calendar invite, a text. It unfurls in Slack, LinkedIn and iMessage with a branded card that carries the **Link Title**.

<Frame>
  ![Public share page with like, comment and repost toggles and a prewritten comment](/images/amplify/share-page.png)
</Frame>

## What recipients see

Whoever opens the link sees your brief, what they are being asked to do, and a three-step **How it works**: connect your LinkedIn account, choose how you want to engage, and that's it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect LinkedIn">
    Recipients connect in one click, directly with LinkedIn. They do not need an Ordinal account or login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to engage">
    For an **Engage with a post** action, recipients toggle like, comment and repost on or off, and edit the comment or repost copy, including @mentions. For a **Publish a post** action, they pick a variant and edit the copy. The media you attached stays as it is.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule">
    Recipients confirm a time in the **Share Post to LinkedIn** modal. A banner shows the scheduled time, with an option to edit it or **Unschedule**. Ordinal publishes at that time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When engagements go out

- **Scheduled post:** engagements are timed after the post goes live and cannot be scheduled before it.
- **Live post:** the share page shows a **Posted** badge with the LinkedIn embed, and engagements default to now.
