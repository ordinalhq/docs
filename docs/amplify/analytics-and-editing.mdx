## Analytics card

A published link shows an **Analytics** card with:

| Metric | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Opens** | How many times the link was opened |
| **Connections** | How many people connected LinkedIn |
| **Likes**, **Comments**, **Reposts** | Engagements completed through an **Engage with a post** action |
| **Posts** | Posts published through a **Publish a post** action |

Only the metrics for the actions you configured appear.

## View activity

Click **View activity** to open a page that lists each person who connected, scheduled or published, one row per action, with a link to their LinkedIn profile.

From an MCP client, `ordinal_list_amplify_engagers` returns the same people, with impressions, likes, comments and shares on the reposts and posts they made. See the [Amplify tools](/mcp/tools#amplify).

## Editing a live link

While a link is live you can change:

- The internal title and the public **Link Title**
- The brief
- The comment, repost and post variants
- The like, comment and repost toggles and the reaction types

On a live link the variant editor has an explicit **Edit** and **Save**. Saving a variant updates the copy for every recipient who has not scheduled yet.

These are locked after publish:

- Adding or removing actions
- Changing the target post
- Changing the URL slug

Completed links are read-only.

## Deleting a link

Right-click a link in the Amplify list and choose **Delete Link**.
