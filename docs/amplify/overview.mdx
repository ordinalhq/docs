Amplify is a link you build in Ordinal and send to anyone. Whoever opens it connects LinkedIn once, picks how they want to help, and their likes, comments, reposts or posts go out when your post does. Open **Amplify** in the sidebar to build one.

## Who it is for

Amplify is for marketing teams and the people who run exec, launch and event posts. One link replaces the Slack message that says "would love some engagement on this" and the chasing that follows it.

Recipients need a LinkedIn account only. They do not need an Ordinal seat or a login.

## What a link can do

A link holds one or more actions. You can combine both kinds in the same link.

| Action | What recipients do |
| --- | --- |
| **Engage with a post** | Like, comment on or repost one of your LinkedIn posts, either a post scheduled in Ordinal or a post that is already live |
| **Publish a post** | Post one of your copy variants from their own LinkedIn profile |

Engagements on a scheduled post are timed after the post goes live, so nobody can like a post that does not exist yet.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Build the link">
    Add an **Engage with a post** or **Publish a post** action, write the variants, add a brief and labels, then click **Create Amplify Link** to get a short link. See [Building a link](/amplify/building-a-link).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send it anywhere">
    Share the link in Slack, email, a calendar invite or a text. Recipients connect LinkedIn, choose their engagements or edit the post, and schedule. See [Sharing a link](/amplify/sharing-a-link).
  </Step>
  <Step title="See who did what">
    Ordinal publishes every engagement at the chosen time, after your post is live. The **Analytics** card and **View activity** page show opens, connections and every action by name. See [Analytics and editing](/amplify/analytics-and-editing).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Draft, Live and Completed

The Amplify list is split into **Draft**, **Live** and **Completed** links. Search the list, or narrow it with the **Created By** filter, which stays applied the next time you open Amplify. Right-click a link to **Delete Link**.

## Amplify with MCP

From Claude or any other MCP client you can search your Amplify links by state, label or creator, with each action's content, and list who engaged on a link, with impressions, likes, comments and shares on the reposts and posts they made. The tools need [MCP access](/mcp/introduction) (Pro and above) and Amplify enabled on your workspace.

See the [Amplify tools](/mcp/tools#amplify) for the full list.
